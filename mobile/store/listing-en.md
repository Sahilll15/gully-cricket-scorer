## Full description

```
Score your gully match ball by ball, right from the pitch. No login, no sign-up. Open the app and start the first ball in under 30 seconds.

Built for how gully cricket is really played
• Gully classic: one tip one hand, last man stands, hit out of the ground is out
• Tip and run, compulsory run, box cricket with wall runs, pairs and double wicket
• Sixes only, tape ball with free hits, office corridor rules, 100 balls, French cricket and Test
• Solo mode when there are no teams: everyone bats on their own and everyone else bowls
• Set your own rules: overs, balls per over, retire at a score, no lbw, six is out, wides and no-balls

Fast scoring with one thumb
• Big keypad for 0 to 6, wide, no ball, bye, leg bye and out
• Say it instead: "four", "wide", "out" or "undo" with voice scoring
• Undo and redo any ball, fix a ball later, swap strike, add a late player
• A full-screen moment when someone hits a six

Share the match live
• Start live sharing and your friends follow every ball with a code or a WhatsApp link
• Phone battery dying? Move scoring to another phone in one tap and take it back later
• Send the scorecard, a poster or a PDF to the group when it's over

Settle the arguments
• Gully Book: records, milestones and bragging rights across every match
• Kitty: who paid for the ball, the tape and the chai, and who owes whom
• Fair play: a neutral umpire, a change log of every rule change, ball lost and player left

Everything you need after the match
• Full scorecard, wagon wheel, run rate charts and player of the match
• Leaderboards for runs, wickets, strike rate, sixes and fielding
• Tournaments with fixtures, a points table and net run rate, plus best-of series
• Plan the next game: day, place and who's in, shared to the group

Your matches stay on your phone. Sign in with your number only if you want a cloud backup to get everything back on a new phone. You can delete your account any time from the app.

Free, with small ads on the home, history, stats and result screens. Never on the live scoring screen.
```
